HELLGATE
A one-switch corridor shooter. The crosshair sweeps on its own — press your switch to fire when it lines up with a demon. A tone sounds whenever the crosshair is locked on. Clear the room and you march deeper.
Works with any single switch that sends Space, Enter, a mouse click, or a screen tap. Menus scan automatically — press when your choice lights up. Any key on the keyboard also works as the switch. Quick tap = select · hold 2s = back · hold 10s = home.
