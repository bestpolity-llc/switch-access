Welcome to Switch Access

These tools let someone use a computer or iPad with just one switch. A switch can be a button, a tap, or any key on a keyboard.

Tap or press any key to continue
👆

On iPad (Touch)

The whole screen is your switch. No hunting for buttons.

👆
Quick Tap
→ Pick something / Do it
✋
Hold 2 Seconds
→ Cancel / Go back
✋
Hold 10 Seconds
→ Go to the main menu
⌨️

On a Computer

Press any key to make a choice.
Press Escape to go back.

That's it. Space, Enter, letters — they all work.

⌨️
Any key = Select
Except Escape. Escape = Back.
