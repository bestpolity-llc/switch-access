SwitchMate Solitaire Privacy Policy

Best Polity LLC · Last updated August 30, 2026
The short version: SwitchMate Solitaire has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no account requirement. The Fire app does not use analytics or behavioral tracking. Game preferences are kept on the device.

1. Information stored on the device

The app stores only the preferences needed to operate the game, such as:

  • Switch-input mode
  • Audio and voice preference
  • Solitaire play mode and scanning speed
  • Temporary browser storage needed to run the current game

This information remains on the device unless the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

2. Network delivery information

The app retrieves the Solitaire game from Best Polity's website. As with any website request, the hosting, security, and content-delivery services involved may receive ordinary technical information such as an IP address, device or browser type, requested file, and timestamp. This information is used to deliver and protect the service, not for advertising or behavioral profiling.

The standalone Fire app blocks the website's optional Firebase and page-view analytics scripts.

3. Purchases

Amazon processes the Appstore purchase. Best Polity LLC does not receive or store the purchaser's card number or payment credentials. Amazon's handling of purchase information is governed by Amazon's own terms and privacy policy.

4. Support messages

The in-game support control opens the user's chosen email application. We receive an email address and message only when a user deliberately sends a support request. Support messages are used to answer the request and maintain the app.

5. Information the app does not collect

  • No precise or approximate location
  • No contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, or health information
  • No advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking
  • No account credentials
  • No game-content analytics in the standalone Fire app

6. Children

The app can be used without an account and does not ask a player to provide personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent, caregiver, teacher, or therapist should handle any support correspondence for a child.

7. Data deletion

On-device information can be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To request deletion of a support email or related support record, contact us from the same email address used for the request.
