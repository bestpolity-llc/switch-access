# SwitchMate for Providers — One-Page Pitch & Spec

**A single-switch activity system for day-hab, respite, and supported-employment programs.**

SwitchMate turns a participant's plan goals into documented, goal-directed activities — run by staff or by the participant themselves — and captures the billing and narrative documentation automatically.

---

## The problem

Direct-support staff are asked to deliver meaningful, goal-directed activities *and* write them all down for billing and progress notes. The documentation is often a second, unpaid job that takes longer than the activity itself. It's the #1 source of administrative fatigue, and it's error-prone when reconstructed from memory hours later.

## The solution

SwitchMate documents the activity **while it happens**, so the note is accurate and free.

1. **Add a participant** and list their plan goals (making friends, learning to type, playing games…).
2. **Build a session** — pick goals + a duration (e.g. "20 minutes"). SwitchMate maps each goal to a real, single-switch-accessible activity and sequences them.
3. **Run it two ways:**
   - **Staff-led** — a guided runner with a per-goal countdown timer and launch buttons.
   - **Participant-led** — generate an invite link; the participant completes it on their own device with **no sign-in**.
4. **Get the documentation** — per-goal completion, time on task, and a written narrative, captured automatically.

---

## What it produces (billing & narrative)

```
John participated in a 20-minute, 3-goal activity session.
✓ Make a friend (choose a conversation topic) — 7m 12s
✓ Practice typing my name — 6m 40s
✓ Play a computer game — 6m 08s
Facilitated by staff. Participant completed the session on their device.
```

Sessions are stored per participant, searchable in the portal, and exportable for your billing/notes workflow.

## The activities (single-switch, no motor dexterity required)

| Goal type | Example activity |
|-----------|------------------|
| Making friends / social | Two-option scanning choice & conversation game |
| Learning to type / communication | AAC keyboard with word prediction |
| Playing games / motor & engagement | Pop It! balloons, Game Maker, Hellgate, Solitaire |
| Life skills / routine | Structured choice-making |

Every activity is operable with **a single switch, a tap, or any key** — designed for people with developmental disabilities.

---

## Privacy & security (why you can trust it)

- **No participant sign-in.** Sessions use secure invite links; participants never create accounts.
- **Data is scoped** per staff member and per organization.
- **Staff access is allowlisted** (approved email / Google sign-in only).
- **On-device data stays on-device** — typing and word prediction never leave the device.
- **No ads, ever.** No selling or sharing personal data.
